# Snooze / archive: design

OpenCode Desktop **2.2.5**, GUI-only plugin. No app modifications.

The vertical session sidebar ends with collapsed **Snoozed** and **Archived** groups. Click or use Enter/Space to toggle; Right/Left expands/collapses. The groups sit inside the host's sidebar scroll region (`vertical-tabs-scroll`, after the tab list), so live rows, Snoozed and Archived scroll as one and the host's edge fades cover all of it; a group never scrolls on its own. While the list is short the groups sit at the bottom. Expanding a group never scrolls and never moves a live row: the open block grows upward into the empty space above it first (the groups below the gap move up with it), and once that space is used up the page grows downward and you scroll to see more.

- **Snooze** is a real submenu of the tab right-click menu (a **Snooze ▸** item with the host chevron; the presets open beside it on hover, Right, Enter or Space; Left/Esc close only the submenu). From the palette, **Snooze session…** opens the same presets anchored under the session's own tab row (fallback: the tab list), and Esc returns focus to that tab. Presets, in the app's `menu-v2` markup: *In 1 hour*, *In 3 hours*, *This evening* (only while 18:00 is ahead), *Tomorrow* 9:00, *Next week* Mon 9:00, then **Custom…**, each with its resolved time right-aligned in the user's locale and 12/24-hour preference (`Intl.DateTimeFormat`), recomputed every time the menu opens. Arrows, Home/End, Enter, Esc work. "In N hours" is elapsed time; Tomorrow/This evening/Next week are local wall-clock times (`src/time.ts`): a time that does not exist (spring forward) moves ahead by the jump, an ambiguous one (fall back) takes the first occurrence; tested in Europe/London, America/New_York, Asia/Kolkata, Asia/Kathmandu, Australia/Lord_Howe, Pacific/Auckland. Next week on a Monday is the following Monday.
- **Custom…** opens a host `Dialog` with a Duration | Date & time switch (host `Button`s). Duration: chips (15m…1w) plus a host `TextInput` parsing `90m`, `1h30`, `1.5h`, `2 days`, `in 2 hours`, `45`; Date & time: a month grid (arrow/Home/End/PageUp/PageDown, today dot, past and >1 year days disabled, locale first weekday via `Intl.Locale`) and a time field (`9:30`, `930`, `2:30 pm`, ↑/↓ ±5 min, Shift ±1 h). The host has no calendar or time field, so the grid is a small bespoke piece in `src/custom.ts` (native buttons, `--v2` tokens). A live line shows the resolved wake time with the zone (`GMT+1 · Europe/London`), a warning with the applied resolution for nonexistent/ambiguous times, and clear errors for past times and anything beyond one year. Enter confirms, Esc cancels.
- The absolute deadline is authoritative: if the device zone or offset changes, the wake time is shown in the current zone and the deadline does not move. One timeout at the nearest deadline plus a `visibilitychange`/`focus` recheck handle sleep, resume and clock changes; deadlines already past at launch wake immediately.
- Snooze is persisted **on this device**, shared between its windows, keyed by server + session. Closing the shell tab does not stop server work. Pending approval/question in the session or known descendants declines snooze. Wake/Undo reopens it. Opening through Home/Recently closed also wakes it.
- Wakes early on approval/question, new execution/queued input, completion or failure. Startup/reconnect checks metadata and pending requests; the deadline timeout and the focus/visibility recheck handle deadlines, resume and missed state changes. Timed wake waits for a disconnected server to reconnect. App-closed deadlines are handled on the next launch, not by a background service.
- **Woke mark.** A session that wakes by itself (deadline, approval/question, new work, completion; also deadlines that passed while the app was closed) shows a clock in its vertical tab's trailing status slot until it is opened. Opening means it becomes the mounted session by any route (click, palette, keyboard, Home), not hover. Woken by Wake/Undo, Home or Recently closed, or while already open, it is not marked; archiving, deleting or re-snoozing it clears the mark. The mark is stored on this device in its own `woke` store, keyed by server + session (capped at 200; an entry for a session deleted while the app was closed is inert). The clock is `src/woke.ts`: the drawn 14px icon, the unread dot's blue (`--v2-background-bg-accent`), a sibling of the host's aria-hidden `tab-status` with `role="img"` and `aria-label="Woke from snooze"`, same slot position, hover swap for the close button and title fade as the native marks, no animation (native marks have none). Precedence: a pending approval/question (`data-status="attention"`) still shows its native mark; the clock replaces the busy ring and unread dot. The host marks carry no tooltip and hide on row hover, so there is none either. While the clock shows it owns the slot: sidebar-repos' last-activity label (`[data-sidebar-time]`, same slot) is hidden and the title fade is the host's 30px, so the two never overlap (the label returns on the open tab and under a pending approval/question, where the clock is hidden). Vertical tab layout only. A session restored as the active tab at launch counts as opened.
- **One hover action, the rest in right-click.** A session tab shows a single **Archive** `IconButton` on hover/focus, beside the app's own close X (untouched: that is "close tab"). Everywhere the app shows a session **Delete…** menu, a **Snooze** submenu and **Archive** sit directly above it, ahead of the separator that precedes it (`src/sessionmenu.ts`): the tab right-click menu, the session header **…** menu (the routed session) and the Home session row menu (that row's session; the server is the connected one that knows the id). The menu is recognised by its shape (last item alone after a separator), not its label. Archived sessions keep their own Restore/Delete; subagents get no Snooze; a draft's items are disabled. The inbox entry menu has no DOM hook for its session and already has a native Archive, so it is untouched. The plugin adds no second Delete. Snoozed and Archived rows are the app's session-tab markup; hover shows one action (**Wake** / **Restore**) and right-click (or the menu key) opens a popup in the same `menu-v2` markup: Wake, Archive, **Delete…** for snoozed; Restore, **Delete…** for archived. Plugin Delete always asks first, says it is permanent, focuses Cancel and calls `client.session.remove({ sessionID })`; on failure an error toast shows and the row stays. There is no session-header cluster. Palette commands (Snooze session…, Archive session, Delete session…) remain.
- **Snooze icon.** The host catalog has no clock, so `src/icons.ts` draws one (16px, outline, `currentColor`, round caps, circle r6 plus hands) and the Snoozed group header uses it. Host `IconButton`/`ctx.ui.Menu` only take catalog names and the native menu's items carry no icons, so the clock appears where we render the markup.
- **Motion = the host's (decided by the user).** Group content shows and hides in one frame (a closed panel is `display: none` and `inert`); only the chevron rotates, `transform .15s` (CSS default `ease`). Rows are added and removed in one frame, with no enter or leave state and no timers. Evidence, OpenCode 2.2.5 `main-*.css`: `[data-component=collapsible] [data-slot=collapsible-content]` has no height or opacity transition, and `[data-slot=collapsible-arrow-icon]` is `transition: transform .15s` (rotate -90deg → 0), as are the Questions, session-review and turn-diff chevrons. The earlier design (grid-template-rows 200ms + opacity + row `translateY(-4px)`, `cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1)`) was removed. Group growth keeps its layout rule (the groups sit at the bottom and grow upward while the list is short, then downward) and does not scroll the sidebar.
- **Reading archived sessions.** Clicking an Archived row (or Enter on its focused link) opens the session in a tab via `ctx.tabs.open(…, { focus: true })` and leaves it archived: no `session.update` call. Only the **Archive** action closes a tab, at the moment it archives; refresh, `session.archived` events and the archived inventory never close tabs, so a tab opened from the group, Home or Recently closed stays. Restore (hover button, or right-click) is the only unarchive. The 2.2.5 sources have no archived guard on the session route or tab (no banner, read-only composer or redirect; "archived" is checked only when resolving a transcript-mentioned session, Home lookup and the palette index), so the session should render as any other, with no archived marker; the host offers none and none is invented. Unverified live.
- **Archive session** uses native `session.update({ archived: true })`, then verifies native state. **Restore** uses `archived: false`, verifies, and opens the session. Archive state is never invented locally.
- **Pages.** Each group shows its first 10 rows. Below them a **Show N more** row (the sidebar's own secondary row, as "New session": a chevron and a muted label; N is 25, or what remains when that is fewer) adds the next 25, until none are left and the button goes away. Pages stay loaded and shown when a group is collapsed. **Archived loads lazily**: nothing is fetched until the group is first expanded, which asks for 10 (`session.list { archived: true, parentID: null, limit: 10 }`); each press asks the server for the next 25 by cursor, only when the rows loaded do not already cover it, and nothing is fetched in the background. Only the visible window is turned into rows, so the DOM stays proportional to what is shown (about 18 nodes per row). The server returns no total, so the Archived count is the number loaded with a **+** while a next page exists, and exact once the last page has arrived; it is `—` until the first page is in (so also before the first expand). With several servers the window is their rows merged newest update first, each server keeping its own order. **Snoozed** is on this device: all entries are known, so only rendering is paged and its count is exact.
- **Events keep the window right without refetching.** `session.archived` fetches that one session and inserts it where the server's order puts it (newest first), or leaves it for the page that holds it when it belongs past the loaded tail; `session.unarchived`, `session.deleted` and Restore/Delete drop the row; `session.renamed` updates a loaded row. A row removed from page 2 pulls the next loaded row in; when that leaves fewer rows than the window, the next page is fetched. Rows are keyed by session id, so none is duplicated or lost across pages. Events before the first expand change nothing (the first page will include them). **Refresh archived sessions** reloads from the first page and refills up to the rows already shown. A failed page shows the error in the group's status line and the button retries. Counts show `—` while loading, disconnected, incompatible or failed. Subagent sessions are never listed.

## Decision: no Archived count

The Archived header shows no number (user decision). The total is known only after the last page loads, so it read `10+` almost always. Snoozed keeps its exact count.

## Decisions: standing down for a host archive UI

Settled with the user ("Yes, small guard now"), 2026-11-01. Option `archive: "auto" | "ours" | "theirs"`, default `auto`, read from `ctx.options` once per setup (a config change reloads the plugin). The decision is `src/guard.ts`; `gui.ts` reads two reactive decisions, `groupDown()` and `actionsDown()` (see below).

- **Two decisions, not one** (fixed 2026-11-01 after the Archived group vanished on 2.2.5). The Archived group is a browser: it lists and restores archived sessions. A host Archive action (a menu item, Home's button) archives but shows nothing archived, so it is no replacement for the group. So in `auto`: **`groupDown`** is true only for a host archived group (`sidebar` signal); **`actionsDown`** is true for any host archive UI (`menu`, `home`, `sidebar`). `theirs` stands both down, `ours` neither.
- **What `groupDown` stands down**: the Archived group (kept mounted and `hidden`, so no flicker and no rebuild; nothing fetched while down; it refills when it returns), the Refresh palette command, and the hiding and sweeping of archived sessions' tabs (the host owns them then). **What `actionsDown` stands down**: our Archive item in the tab, header and Home row menus, the hover Archive button (the host's close X returns), the Archive palette command, and the Archive item on Snoozed rows. **Stays always**: Snooze, the Snoozed group, Delete…, the wake-on-archive and unarchive events, Restore of sessions in view, the woke mark.
- **Detection (auto)**: three signals: a session menu we extend that already has an Archive/Unarchive item (by `data-action`/`data-slot`/`data-value` containing `archive`, or the whole label); a non-ours `data-slot`/`data-component` containing `archived` inside `[data-slot="vertical-tabs-sidebar"]`; Home's `[data-action="home-session-archive"]`. The menu and Home readings are latched until the surface is seen again without the marker (a session menu without the item clears the first; Home rows without the button clear the second); the sidebar reading is live. `ours` and `theirs` skip probing. The sidebar root carries `data-sa-standdown="menu,home,sidebar"` naming the signals that fired (absent when none), so "why is Archived gone" is one attribute read.
- **No keybind signal.** The first version also treated a bound `session.archive`/`session.unarchive` keybind as a host archive command. That was the bug. `ctx.keybinds.keybind(id)` is the host's `Command.keybind(id)`: the registered command's keybind, else the entry in the persisted `command.catalog.v1`, which keeps commands of older builds. An install that ever ran a build with `session.archive` keeps answering `mod+shift+backspace` for it, though 2.2.5 registers no such command (its bundle has no `session.archive` id), so a keybind cannot tell a live host command from a stale one. Reproduced in the harness by adding that catalog entry; the user's real catalog has it (164 entries, against 73 on a fresh install). A host that really adds an archive command also adds a menu item, button or group, which are read directly.
- **Why these and not a registry**: the plugin SDK has no way to list the host's commands, menu items or slots, so the menu is read where we already read it and the sidebar and Home by DOM probe.
- **Cheap and no flicker**: the DOM probe is two `querySelector`s on the sidebar and Home, run in one `requestAnimationFrame` after a mutation under `vertical-tabs-sidebar` (ancestor walk only, so a streaming timeline never wakes it), after a route change, and after each sidebar update. A host group that appears is read before the next paint. The menu reading happens when the menu opens, before our item would be added.
- **Not a trigger**: the experimental Inbox layout (`sidebar-inbox`) has its own Archived section and Archive/Unarchive items, and archives through `session.update({ archived })`. It is another layout: our groups and tab buttons do not exist there, and its menu is not one we extend.
- **API preference**: `writeArchived` calls `client.session.archive` / `unarchive` with `{ sessionID }` when the client has them, falls back to `session.update({ archived })` when they are missing or throw, and the result is checked with `session.get`. The argument shape is a guess (the host's own wrapper takes a bare id; generated client methods take an input object), hence the fallback.

Evidence:
- 2.2.5 sources (extracted from the installed app's source maps): the client declares `session.update({ archived })`, `session.list({ archived })` and the events `session.archived` / `session.unarchived`, and no `session.archive` / `unarchive` method. Home's row Archive button is compiled out (`SHOW_HOME_SESSION_ARCHIVE = false`, `home-session-archive`), and its handler rejects "Session archiving is unavailable" under `// TODO: Restore archiving when the V2 client exposes a session archive API`. No registered command id contains `archive` (`session.delete` and `session.rename` exist); the only host Archive menu item is the Inbox layout's. The Side tabs sidebar has no archived group.
- The experimental Inbox layout (`app/src/shell/titlebar/inbox.tsx`, `inbox-model.ts`) lists up to 50 archived sessions in a section and has Archive/Unarchive menu items through `session.update({ archived })`. Its section header has no `data-slot`.
- Upstream commit fa9da987 (2026-10-19, "restore session archiving"): adds `archived` to `session.list` and `session.update`, the two events, and the core service's `Session.archive(sessionID)` / `unarchive(sessionID)`. It adds no client method of the same name, so `session.archive(id)` on the client is still an expectation, not a fact.
- Upstream requests that would add the UI: #60581 (collapsible project groups and an archive filter in the session sidebar), #59220 (project grouping, filtering and pinned sessions in the sidebar inbox), #60867 (independent Side bar mode).
- Harness (private root, 2.2.5, Side tabs): `data-sa-archive="ours"` with Snoozed and Archived groups, no host marker, no Home archive button (Home rows present), the real tab menu `Rename, Close tab, Snooze, Archive, Delete session…`; with `archive: "theirs"` in the config the group hid with no reload, and removing it brought it back. No host UI was faked.

## Compatibility / verification boundary

Commands, data, storage, toasts, dialogs and native mutations use the public SDK. 2.2.5 has no sidebar-bottom slot: the two group nodes and a parent layout attribute are injected next to `[data-slot="vertical-tabs"]`, one Archive button is appended as a sibling inside each `[data-tab-key]` tab wrapper (marked `data-sa-tab-host`, removed on cleanup), and the shared observer (`src/hub.ts`) adds our items to the app's tab context menu when it opens (see below). Cleanup removes them. Host title, rename, avatars, status, scroll and footer controls are left intact. Inbox/horizontal tab layouts have no group attachment; commands still work.

The public tab API exposes only user-style `close`, so snoozed/archived tabs can appear in native Recently closed. Restoring an archived entry should use this plugin's Restore action; opening an archive through native history does not itself unarchive it.

Tests cover mocked API calls/errors, page math, event handling across pages, duplicate-free windows, the Show-more label, snooze transitions and DST, the woke mark (set, clear on open, persistence, passed-while-closed, no mark for never-snoozed/Home-opened/archived), keyed group counts/toggles, startup/reconnect, manual wake, undo, and cleanup/reload against a versioned sidebar fixture. Cold permission/form lists and metadata may be undefined despite SDK declarations: new snoozes require confirmed hydrated state; existing snoozes defer safely while known deadline/archive/attention signals remain effective. **Tab-row button position (right offset 30px to clear the close button), header placement, and animations are unverified live. Actual desktop appearance, interaction and native archive writes remain unverified**: native desktop-control tools were unavailable. Mock/fixture tests are not live API or UI evidence.

## Context-menu integration

`MenuItem` registers only in `session.panel`, `server.add` and `server.row`, and `ctx.ui.Menu` is a button-triggered menu with no way to open at a point, so neither can extend or replace a tab context menu. The app's menu (Kobalte, `Menu.Context` in `tab-nav.tsx`) is a portal of `[role=menu][data-component=menu-v2-content]` labelled by the tab link. `src/tabmenu.ts` watches `<body>` for that node, resolves the tab through `aria-labelledby` → `closest([data-tab-key])` (so only session tabs), and `extendNative` in `src/menu.ts` inserts `menu-v2-item` nodes (same attributes, `data-slot`s, `data-variant=critical`) before the app's separator. It is idempotent (`data-sa-menu` marks our nodes), removed on cleanup, and does not touch the app's items. Kobalte keeps a collection of its own items, so arrow/Home/End presses that would step onto or off our items are handled by us (highlight moved to a single item, as Kobalte does); every other key still reaches the app. Choosing an item dispatches Escape on the menu to close it through the app's own path, then runs the action. Tradeoff: this depends on the 2.2.5 DOM, and highlight state for a native item is cleared by us when focus moves onto ours. Snoozed/Archived rows have no native menu, so they use `openMenu` (fixed popup, same markup, arrows/Home/End/Enter/Escape, outside-click and blur close, focus restored).

The Snooze submenu is a `menu-v2-content` element appended inside the native menu element (position absolute, `left: calc(100% + 2px)`, flipping left when there is no room), so Kobalte's outside-click and focus-out checks see it as part of the menu. Its items copy the host's item markup and computed styles match the native items exactly (height 28, padding 0 6px 0 12px, 13px/24px 440, radius 4, hint 11px 530 `rgb(128,128,128)`, menu radius 6, same shadow).

## Decisions: a send resets snooze and archive

Asked by the user, 2026-11-03: "when a session is snoozed or archived and you send a message this should reset the snoozed or archived status."

- **Where it is stored.** Archive is the host's (`session.time.archived`); snooze is this plugin's device store. Host check, harness (OpenCode 2.2.5, private root, stub model): a composer send into an archived session leaves it archived (`time.archived` unchanged), so the host does not do this.
- **Signal: `session.inbox.enqueued`, the one subscription already there.** No polling, no new events. The item is a user message for the composer and for peer, monitor and scheduler wakes alike. The composer stamps `payload.metadata.displayText` (read off real sends in the harness, with `comments`, `attachments`, `agent`, `model`); timeline-rail reads the same field for slash commands, which carry the "/command args" line. The other senders stamp `peerMessage`, `monitor`, `scheduledTask`, `orchestrator` and no `displayText`; a direct API prompt stamps nothing. `isUserSend` (`src/snooze.ts`) accepts only `type: "user"` with a string `displayText`, so an unknown sender is never taken for the user. Queued and steering sends differ only in `delivery`; not exercised live.
- **What a user send does.** Snoozed: removed, as any wake, with the session counted as already opened (no woke mark, whichever session is on screen). Archived: `session.update({ archived: false })` via `setArchived`, verified with `session.get`, then the loaded Archived window drops the row; no tab is opened or focused (the user is in it). Woke mark: an explicit send clears it (the user has engaged), snoozed or not.
- **Unchanged, from the code:** a snooze wakes on any inbox delivery, execution start or finish, approval or question, whoever caused it. So a monitor, scheduler or peer message into a snoozed session wakes it, marked (harness: Snoozed 1 → 0, clock mark shown), as the Decisions above always said ("new execution/queued input"). Those messages, and assistant output, never unarchive (harness: scheduler-stamped prompt into an archived session left it archived).
- **Loops and windows.** Our own writes emit `session.unarchived`, which only drops the row, never an inbox item. Every window sees the event: each reads its store, calls only if the session is archived, and guards with the per-session action key, so a double delivery writes once in a window; windows may both write, which the server accepts (idempotent). Failure: error toast (as other actions), nothing changed locally, the next send retries.
- **A successful snooze is silent.** No "Snoozed on this device" toast: the row leaving the list and appearing in the Snoozed group is the confirmation (user: remove it). Failures still toast. The toast also carried the only Undo; undo is now Wake on the Snoozed row or the Wake command. Wakes by deadline or by activity keep their woke mark.
- **Evidence (harness, workspace Z):** composer send into an archived session: unarchived about 0.4 s later; one frame moves the row from Archived to the live list, group header positions step once by the removed row, no frame with the session in both or neither, Archived header carries no count. Snoozed 1 → 0 with the live row appearing in the same frame and the group tops unchanged. A real composer send into a snoozed session cannot be made in the UI: opening a snoozed session by any route already wakes it; that path is covered by unit tests and by a composer-shaped prompt through the API. Two windows, slash commands and queued/steering sends: not verified live.

## Decisions: DOM hooks

- **Sidebar groups, tab-row actions and the session-menu items** are DOM: tab rows and the session menus have no extension point ([host-architecture.md §8](../../../.claude/skills/opencode-gui-plugins/reference/host-architecture.md#8-public-and-internal-boundary)). The palette commands and the stored snoozes use public APIs (`Command`, `Store.global`).
- **Menu markup** is the host's, adapted; it is why the plugin tracks 2.2.5 markup.
- **One observer, scoped** (`src/hub.ts`). The old plugin ran five whole-document observers (guard, session menus, tab rows, woke marks, sidebar), each with its own queue. There is no official hook for tab rows, session menus or the sidebar's scroll region, so they stay DOM reads, but one `MutationObserver` serves all five: the vertical sidebar subtree, the row that holds `<main>` (children only, to find a sidebar that mounts later), `<body>`'s direct children (a menu or dialog portal is seen when it mounts, then watched until it goes). The app root and its streaming timeline are never observed. Records are filtered once (our own nodes dropped), each consumer pre-filters the rest, and sidebar work runs in one animation frame; menus are extended in the callback, before they paint. Evidence: DOM probe in the harness (Kobalte portals are fresh `body` children; the sidebar `aside` is a child of a row that survives the Top/Side tabs switch) and the Performance table below.
- **No polling.** The 30 s `recheck` is gone. Events keep every connected snooze current, so a server is asked again (`session.sync` plus permissions and forms per snooze) only after the window was away 60 s or more, or a deadline timeout fired 60 s late (sleep). Focus and visibility changes check deadlines only. One `setTimeout` waits for the nearest deadline and none exists while nothing is snoozed; an interval relabels "in 2h 5m" only while the Snoozed group is open.
- **Owner-bound cleanup.** Every observer, listener, timer and subscription registers its `onCleanup` where it is acquired, so a setup that throws midway releases what it took (a returned cleanup is never run by the host in that case). The setup returns nothing. `archivedClosing` timeouts are cleared there too.
- **Two sidebar effects, as memo + `createKeyed`.** The Snoozed rows and the Archived window are built by separate memos, so a clock tick or a snooze change no longer rebuilds up to 200 archived rows. A row's "viewing" mark is read in the row's own scope (`active()`), so a session switch re-marks two rows instead of rebuilding the group.

- Observer callbacks while a reply streams or the timeline scrolls: **bare level** (before: 6x and 5x). Observers the plugin creates: 5 whole-document → 1 scoped (`hub.ts` test counts it).
- Heap after forced GC 113 to 118 MB in every condition (bare 114.5), listeners 8.4k to 8.9k, DOM nodes: plugin adds about 3.4k (the 210 archived rows, about 15 per row, bounded by what "Show more" asked for). No growth across the three iterations.
- **Switch: 2.95 ms/s of plugin JS** (26 ms for 20 switches, 1.3 ms each: one hub flush, the tab-title effect, tab-row sync) is 0.3% of the main thread but 29% of bare scripting (10.2 ms/s). Absolute, it is far inside a frame; against the literal "within 3% of bare scripting time" it fails. Not reduced further: the work is the 37-row `querySelectorAll` sync, per frame, that a tab change legitimately needs. Open question for the bar's reading.
- Menus open with 11 slow (>20 ms) frames against 1 bare (before: 8): adding the Snooze submenu and Archive item to a menu the host just laid out. Unchanged by this work.
- Whole-process scripting deltas between conditions (e.g. scroll 93 → 160 ms/s) are noise from the `bare` baseline (its three scroll runs were 137, 64, 93) and from 4k more DOM nodes, not plugin JS: profiled plugin time in scroll is 0.4 ms/s.
- Not measured: a real model stream, two windows, light/dark, 5,000 archived rows. Idle was measured with snoozes present but the group closed.

Standing cost now: one observer (zero callbacks while nothing in the sidebar or a portal changes), one deadline `setTimeout` while snoozes exist, an interval only while the Snoozed group is open.
# Snooze / archive: design

OpenCode Desktop **2.2.5**, GUI-only plugin. No app modifications.

The vertical session sidebar ends with collapsed **Snoozed** and **Archived** groups. Click or use Enter/Space to toggle; Right/Left expands/collapses. The groups sit inside the host's sidebar scroll region (`vertical-tabs-scroll`, after the tab list), so live rows, Snoozed and Archived scroll as one and the host's edge fades cover all of it; a group never scrolls on its own. While the list is short the groups sit at the bottom. Expanding a group never scrolls and never moves a live row: the open block grows upward into the empty space above it first (the groups below the gap move up with it), and once that space is used up the page grows downward and you scroll to see more.

- **Snooze** is a real submenu of the tab right-click menu (a **Snooze ▸** item with the host chevron; the presets open beside it on hover, Right, Enter or Space; Left/Esc close only the submenu). From the palette, **Snooze session…** opens the same presets anchored under the session's own tab row (fallback: the tab list), and Esc returns focus to that tab. Presets, in the app's `menu-v2` markup: *In 1 hour*, *In 3 hours*, *This evening* (only while 18:00 is ahead), *Tomorrow* 9:00, *Next week* Mon 9:00, then **Custom…**, each with its resolved time right-aligned in the user's locale and 12/24-hour preference (`Intl.DateTimeFormat`), recomputed every time the menu opens. Arrows, Home/End, Enter, Esc work. "In N hours" is elapsed time; Tomorrow/This evening/Next week are local wall-clock times (`src/time.ts`): a time that does not exist (spring forward) moves ahead by the jump, an ambiguous one (fall back) takes the first occurrence; tested in Europe/London, America/New_York, Asia/Kolkata, Asia/Kathmandu, Australia/Lord_Howe, Pacific/Auckland. Next week on a Monday is the following Monday.
- **Custom…** opens a host `Dialog` with a Duration | Date & time switch (host `Button`s). Duration: chips (15m…1w) plus a host `TextInput` parsing `90m`, `1h30`, `1.5h`, `2 days`, `in 2 hours`, `45`; Date & time: a month grid (arrow/Home/End/PageUp/PageDown, today dot, past and >1 year days disabled, locale first weekday via `Intl.Locale`) and a time field (`9:30`, `930`, `2:30 pm`, ↑/↓ ±5 min, Shift ±1 h). The host has no calendar or time field, so the grid is a small bespoke piece in `src/custom.ts` (native buttons, `--v2` tokens). A live line shows the resolved wake time with the zone (`GMT+1 · Europe/London`), a warning with the applied resolution for nonexistent/ambiguous times, and clear errors for past times and anything beyond one year. Enter confirms, Esc cancels.
- The absolute deadline is authoritative: if the device zone or offset changes, the wake time is shown in the current zone and the deadline does not move. One timeout at the nearest deadline plus a `visibilitychange`/`focus` recheck handle sleep, resume and clock changes; deadlines already past at launch wake immediately.
- Snooze is persisted **on this device**, shared between its windows, keyed by server + session. Closing the shell tab does not stop server work. Pending approval/question in the session or known descendants declines snooze. Wake/Undo reopens it. Opening through Home/Recently closed also wakes it.
- Wakes early on approval/question, new execution/queued input, completion or failure. Startup/reconnect checks metadata and pending requests; the deadline timeout and the focus/visibility recheck handle deadlines, resume and missed state changes. Timed wake waits for a disconnected server to reconnect. App-closed deadlines are handled on the next launch, not by a background service.
- **Woke mark.** A session that wakes by itself (deadline, approval/question, new work, completion; also deadlines that passed while the app was closed) shows a clock in its vertical tab's trailing status slot until it is opened. Opening means it becomes the mounted session by any route (click, palette, keyboard, Home), not hover. Woken by Wake/Undo, Home or Recently closed, or while already open, it is not marked; archiving, deleting or re-snoozing it clears the mark. The mark is stored on this device in its own `woke` store, keyed by server + session (capped at 200; an entry for a session deleted while the app was closed is inert). The clock is `src/woke.ts`: the drawn 14px icon, the unread dot's blue (`--v2-background-bg-accent`), a sibling of the host's aria-hidden `tab-status` with `role="img"` and `aria-label="Woke from snooze"`, same slot position, hover swap for the close button and title fade as the native marks, no animation (native marks have none). Precedence: a pending approval/question (`data-status="attention"`) still shows its native mark; the clock replaces the busy ring and unread dot. The host marks carry no tooltip and hide on row hover, so there is none either. While the clock shows it owns the slot: sidebar-repos' last-activity label (`[data-sidebar-time]`, same slot) is hidden and the title fade is the host's 30px, so the two never overlap (the label returns on the open tab and under a pending approval/question, where the clock is hidden). Vertical tab layout only. A session restored as the active tab at launch counts as opened.
- **One hover action, the rest in right-click.** A session tab shows a single **Archive** `IconButton` on hover/focus, beside the app's own close X (untouched: that is "close tab"). Everywhere the app shows a session **Delete…** menu, a **Snooze** submenu and **Archive** sit directly above it, ahead of the separator that precedes it (`src/sessionmenu.ts`): the tab right-click menu, the session header **…** menu (the routed session) and the Home session row menu (that row's session; the server is the connected one that knows the id). The menu is recognised by its shape (last item alone after a separator), not its label. Archived sessions keep their own Restore/Delete; subagents get no Snooze; a draft's items are disabled. The inbox entry menu has no DOM hook for its session and already has a native Archive, so it is untouched. The plugin adds no second Delete. Snoozed and Archived rows are the app's session-tab markup; hover shows one action (**Wake** / **Restore**) and right-click (or the menu key) opens a popup in the same `menu-v2` markup: Wake, Archive, **Delete…** for snoozed; Restore, **Delete…** for archived. Plugin Delete always asks first, says it is permanent, focuses Cancel and calls `client.session.remove({ sessionID })`; on failure an error toast shows and the row stays. There is no session-header cluster. Palette commands (Snooze session…, Archive session, Delete session…) remain.
- **Snooze icon.** The host catalog has no clock, so `src/icons.ts` draws one (16px, outline, `currentColor`, round caps, circle r6 plus hands) and the Snoozed group header uses it. Host `IconButton`/`ctx.ui.Menu` only take catalog names and the native menu's items carry no icons, so the clock appears where we render the markup.
- **Motion = the host's (decided by the user).** Group content shows and hides in one frame (a closed panel is `display: none` and `inert`); only the chevron rotates, `transform .15s` (CSS default `ease`). Rows are added and removed in one frame, with no enter or leave state and no timers. Evidence, OpenCode 2.2.5 `main-*.css`: `[data-component=collapsible] [data-slot=collapsible-content]` has no height or opacity transition, and `[data-slot=collapsible-arrow-icon]` is `transition: transform .15s` (rotate -90deg → 0), as are the Questions, session-review and turn-diff chevrons. The earlier design (grid-template-rows 200ms + opacity + row `translateY(-4px)`, `cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1)`) was removed. Group growth keeps its layout rule (the groups sit at the bottom and grow upward while the list is short, then downward) and does not scroll the sidebar.
- **Reading archived sessions.** Clicking an Archived row (or Enter on its focused link) opens the session in a tab via `ctx.tabs.open(…, { focus: true })` and leaves it archived: no `session.update` call. Only the **Archive** action closes a tab, at the moment it archives; refresh, `session.archived` events and the archived inventory never close tabs, so a tab opened from the group, Home or Recently closed stays. Restore (hover button, or right-click) is the only unarchive. The 2.2.5 sources have no archived guard on the session route or tab (no banner, read-only composer or redirect; "archived" is checked only when resolving a transcript-mentioned session, Home lookup and the palette index), so the session should render as any other, with no archived marker; the host offers none and none is invented. Unverified live.
- **Archive session** uses native `session.update({ archived: true })`, then verifies native state. **Restore** uses `archived: false`, verifies, and opens the session. Archive state is never invented locally.
- **Pages.** Each group shows its first 10 rows. Below them a **Show N more** row (the sidebar's own secondary row, as "New session": a chevron and a muted label; N is 25, or what remains when that is fewer) adds the next 25, until none are left and the button goes away. Pages stay loaded and shown when a group is collapsed. **Archived loads lazily**: nothing is fetched until the group is first expanded, which asks for 10 (`session.list { archived: true, parentID: null, limit: 10 }`); each press asks the server for the next 25 by cursor, only when the rows loaded do not already cover it, and nothing is fetched in the background. Only the visible window is turned into rows, so the DOM stays proportional to what is shown (about 18 nodes per row). The server returns no total, so the Archived count is the number loaded with a **+** while a next page exists, and exact once the last page has arrived; it is `—` until the first page is in (so also before the first expand). With several servers the window is their rows merged newest update first, each server keeping its own order. **Snoozed** is on this device: all entries are known, so only rendering is paged and its count is exact.
- **Events keep the window right without refetching.** `session.archived` fetches that one session and inserts it where the server's order puts it (newest first), or leaves it for the page that holds it when it belongs past the loaded tail; `session.unarchived`, `session.deleted` and Restore/Delete drop the row; `session.renamed` updates a loaded row. A row removed from page 2 pulls the next loaded row in; when that leaves fewer rows than the window, the next page is fetched. Rows are keyed by session id, so none is duplicated or lost across pages. Events before the first expand change nothing (the first page will include them). **Refresh archived sessions** reloads from the first page and refills up to the rows already shown. A failed page shows the error in the group's status line and the button retries. Counts show `—` while loading, disconnected, incompatible or failed. Subagent sessions are never listed.

## Decisions: standing down for a host archive UI

Settled with the user ("Yes, small guard now"), 2026-11-01. Option `archive: "auto" | "ours" | "theirs"`, default `auto`, read from `ctx.options` once per setup (a config change reloads the plugin). The decision is `src/guard.ts`; `gui.ts` reads two reactive decisions, `groupDown()` and `actionsDown()` (see below).

- **Two decisions, not one** (fixed 2026-11-01 after the Archived group vanished on 2.2.5). The Archived group is a browser: it lists and restores archived sessions. A host Archive action (a menu item, Home's button) archives but shows nothing archived, so it is no replacement for the group. So in `auto`: **`groupDown`** is true only for a host archived group (`sidebar` signal); **`actionsDown`** is true for any host archive UI (`menu`, `home`, `sidebar`). `theirs` stands both down, `ours` neither.
- **What `groupDown` stands down**: the Archived group (kept mounted and `hidden`, so no flicker and no rebuild; nothing fetched while down; it refills when it returns), the Refresh palette command, and the hiding and sweeping of archived sessions' tabs (the host owns them then). **What `actionsDown` stands down**: our Archive item in the tab, header and Home row menus, the hover Archive button (the host's close X returns), the Archive palette command, and the Archive item on Snoozed rows. **Stays always**: Snooze, the Snoozed group, Delete…, the wake-on-archive and unarchive events, Restore of sessions in view, the woke mark.
- **Detection (auto)**: three signals: a session menu we extend that already has an Archive/Unarchive item (by `data-action`/`data-slot`/`data-value` containing `archive`, or the whole label); a non-ours `data-slot`/`data-component` containing `archived` inside `[data-slot="vertical-tabs-sidebar"]`; Home's `[data-action="home-session-archive"]`. The menu and Home readings are latched until the surface is seen again without the marker (a session menu without the item clears the first; Home rows without the button clear the second); the sidebar reading is live. `ours` and `theirs` skip probing. The sidebar root carries `data-sa-standdown="menu,home,sidebar"` naming the signals that fired (absent when none), so "why is Archived gone" is one attribute read.
- **No keybind signal.** The first version also treated a bound `session.archive`/`session.unarchive` keybind as a host archive command. That was the bug. `ctx.keybinds.keybind(id)` is the host's `Command.keybind(id)`: the registered command's keybind, else the entry in the persisted `command.catalog.v1`, which keeps commands of older builds. An install that ever ran a build with `session.archive` keeps answering `mod+shift+backspace` for it, though 2.2.5 registers no such command (its bundle has no `session.archive` id), so a keybind cannot tell a live host command from a stale one. Reproduced in the harness by adding that catalog entry; the user's real catalog has it (164 entries, against 73 on a fresh install). A host that really adds an archive command also adds a menu item, button or group, which are read directly.
- **Why these and not a registry**: the plugin SDK has no way to list the host's commands, menu items or slots, so the menu is read where we already read it and the sidebar and Home by DOM probe.
- **Cheap and no flicker**: the DOM probe is two `querySelector`s on the sidebar and Home, run in one `requestAnimationFrame` after a mutation under `vertical-tabs-sidebar` (ancestor walk only, so a streaming timeline never wakes it), after a route change, and after each sidebar update. A host group that appears is read before the next paint. The menu reading happens when the menu opens, before our item would be added.
- **Not a trigger**: the experimental Inbox layout (`sidebar-inbox`) has its own Archived section and Archive/Unarchive items, and archives through `session.update({ archived })`. It is another layout: our groups and tab buttons do not exist there, and its menu is not one we extend.
- **API preference**: `writeArchived` calls `client.session.archive` / `unarchive` with `{ sessionID }` when the client has them, falls back to `session.update({ archived })` when they are missing or throw, and the result is checked with `session.get`. The argument shape is a guess (the host's own wrapper takes a bare id; generated client methods take an input object), hence the fallback.

Evidence:
- 2.2.5 sources (extracted from the installed app's source maps): the client declares `session.update({ archived })`, `session.list({ archived })` and the events `session.archived` / `session.unarchived`, and no `session.archive` / `unarchive` method. Home's row Archive button is compiled out (`SHOW_HOME_SESSION_ARCHIVE = false`, `home-session-archive`), and its handler rejects "Session archiving is unavailable" under `// TODO: Restore archiving when the V2 client exposes a session archive API`. No registered command id contains `archive` (`session.delete` and `session.rename` exist); the only host Archive menu item is the Inbox layout's. The Side tabs sidebar has no archived group.
- The experimental Inbox layout (`app/src/shell/titlebar/inbox.tsx`, `inbox-model.ts`) lists up to 50 archived sessions in a section and has Archive/Unarchive menu items through `session.update({ archived })`. Its section header has no `data-slot`.
- Upstream commit fa9da987 (2026-10-19, "restore session archiving"): adds `archived` to `session.list` and `session.update`, the two events, and the core service's `Session.archive(sessionID)` / `unarchive(sessionID)`. It adds no client method of the same name, so `session.archive(id)` on the client is still an expectation, not a fact.
- Upstream requests that would add the UI: #60581 (collapsible project groups and an archive filter in the session sidebar), #59220 (project grouping, filtering and pinned sessions in the sidebar inbox), #60867 (independent Side bar mode).
- Harness (private root, 2.2.5, Side tabs): `data-sa-archive="ours"` with Snoozed and Archived groups, no host marker, no Home archive button (Home rows present), the real tab menu `Rename, Close tab, Snooze, Archive, Delete session…`; with `archive: "theirs"` in the config the group hid with no reload, and removing it brought it back. No host UI was faked.

## Context-menu integration

`MenuItem` registers only in `session.panel`, `server.add` and `server.row`, and `ctx.ui.Menu` is a button-triggered menu with no way to open at a point, so neither can extend or replace a tab context menu. The app's menu (Kobalte, `Menu.Context` in `tab-nav.tsx`) is a portal of `[role=menu][data-component=menu-v2-content]` labelled by the tab link. `src/tabmenu.ts` watches `<body>` for that node, resolves the tab through `aria-labelledby` → `closest([data-tab-key])` (so only session tabs), and `extendNative` in `src/menu.ts` inserts `menu-v2-item` nodes (same attributes, `data-slot`s, `data-variant=critical`) before the app's separator. It is idempotent (`data-sa-menu` marks our nodes), removed on cleanup, and does not touch the app's items. Kobalte keeps a collection of its own items, so arrow/Home/End presses that would step onto or off our items are handled by us (highlight moved to a single item, as Kobalte does); every other key still reaches the app. Choosing an item dispatches Escape on the menu to close it through the app's own path, then runs the action. Tradeoff: this depends on the 2.2.5 DOM, and highlight state for a native item is cleared by us when focus moves onto ours. Snoozed/Archived rows have no native menu, so they use `openMenu` (fixed popup, same markup, arrows/Home/End/Enter/Escape, outside-click and blur close, focus restored).

The Snooze submenu is a `menu-v2-content` element appended inside the native menu element (position absolute, `left: calc(100% + 2px)`, flipping left when there is no room), so Kobalte's outside-click and focus-out checks see it as part of the menu. Its items copy the host's item markup and computed styles match the native items exactly (height 28, padding 0 6px 0 12px, 13px/24px 440, radius 4, hint 11px 530 `rgb(128,128,128)`, menu radius 6, same shadow).

## Decisions: DOM hooks

- **Sidebar groups, tab-row actions and the session-menu items** are DOM: tab rows and the session menus have no extension point ([host-architecture.md §8](../../../.claude/skills/opencode-gui-plugins/reference/host-architecture.md#8-public-and-internal-boundary)). The palette commands and the stored snoozes use public APIs (`Command`, `Store.global`).
- **Menu markup** is the host's, adapted; it is why the plugin tracks 2.2.5 markup.
- **One observer, scoped** (`src/hub.ts`). The old plugin ran five whole-document observers (guard, session menus, tab rows, woke marks, sidebar), each with its own queue. There is no official hook for tab rows, session menus or the sidebar's scroll region, so they stay DOM reads, but one `MutationObserver` serves all five: the vertical sidebar subtree, the row that holds `<main>` (children only, to find a sidebar that mounts later), `<body>`'s direct children (a menu or dialog portal is seen when it mounts, then watched until it goes). The app root and its streaming timeline are never observed. Records are filtered once (our own nodes dropped), each consumer pre-filters the rest, and sidebar work runs in one animation frame; menus are extended in the callback, before they paint. Evidence: DOM probe in the harness (Kobalte portals are fresh `body` children; the sidebar `aside` is a child of a row that survives the Top/Side tabs switch) and the Performance table below.
- **No polling.** The 30 s `recheck` is gone. Events keep every connected snooze current, so a server is asked again (`session.sync` plus permissions and forms per snooze) only after the window was away 60 s or more, or a deadline timeout fired 60 s late (sleep). Focus and visibility changes check deadlines only. One `setTimeout` waits for the nearest deadline and none exists while nothing is snoozed; an interval relabels "in 2h 5m" only while the Snoozed group is open.
- **Owner-bound cleanup.** Every observer, listener, timer and subscription registers its `onCleanup` where it is acquired, so a setup that throws midway releases what it took (a returned cleanup is never run by the host in that case). The setup returns nothing. `archivedClosing` timeouts are cleared there too.
- **Two sidebar effects, as memo + `createKeyed`.** The Snoozed rows and the Archived window are built by separate memos, so a clock tick or a snooze change no longer rebuilds up to 200 archived rows. A row's "viewing" mark is read in the row's own scope (`active()`), so a session switch re-marks two rows instead of rebuilding the group.

## Decisions: notifications (M5)

Supersede the woke-mark and hub notes above where they differ.

- **The clock is native-ui's tab mark.** `src/woke.ts` is a thin `createTabMarks` call (the clock glyph, owner `snooze-archive`); `src/hub.ts` only names what this plugin injects and builds native-ui's `createDomHub`. The look is unchanged: measured in the harness against the previous build, the mark's box, colour (`--v2-orange-800`), glyph, status-slot swap, `--sidebar-fade` and link mask are identical; only the hooks are renamed, `data-sa-woke` to `data-notify` and `data-sa-woke-mark` to `data-notify-mark` (sidebar-repos lists `data-notify` for the title fade, and its own `data-sa-woke` entry is gone).
- **A wake is published as `info`** (`snooze-archive:woke:<server>/<session>`, title "Woke from snooze", target the session) through `notify()`; with no `notifications` plugin that is silent (`info` has no fallback toast), exactly as before. Opening the session, archiving, deleting or re-snoozing retracts it (`clearNotification`).
- **One mark, never two.** With a sink installed the hub draws the woke mark (its `info` glyph) and this plugin's clock stays away; without one the clock is ours. The tab-mark owner field keeps the two from removing each other's node when the sink appears after the first draw. This is the one place the look differs with the hub installed: the hub's `info` glyph instead of the clock.
- A wake that starts a turn leaves no extra mark: only the no-turn wakes (deadline, `inbox.enqueued`) mark, as before. Waking by the user's own action (Wake, Undo, a send) is silent, as before.
- Tests: a sink receives one `info` with the right key and target and no toast, the clock is not drawn, clearing retracts it; without a sink the clock shows and no toast. The CSS tests read native-ui's `tabMarkCss`.
