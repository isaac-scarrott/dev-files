description: Convene a panel of independent expert sub-agents to critique an artifact, each through a distinct discipline lens. Use when the user wants a multi-angle expert review, a red-team, a second opinion, to poke holes, stress-test a design, or "convene the council" / "jedi council". For first-person user reactions use the-focus-group; for routine diff review use code-review.

Convene a panel of experts to tear into this artifact. Give each expert one discipline lens no other expert holds — security, performance, accessibility, copy, whatever this artifact actually needs. Cast as many as it warrants and no more: two for a README, more for something with real consequences if it's wrong. For a locked-in or high-stakes call, brief one expert to argue the opposite. If the artifact is small enough to answer in a paragraph yourself, do that and don't convene.

Dispatch the experts as sub-agents in parallel, never one after another — an expert who can see another's verdict stops being independent, and the panel collapses into a single voice wearing many hats. Give each their lens, the artifact, and licence to be blunt and cite specifics. If you can't dispatch real independent sub-agents in this environment, stop and tell the user rather than role-play the panel yourself.

Then synthesise honestly, in plain chat with plain names — no report files, no theatrics. Convergence only counts when the experts were independent; a point only one expert raised can be the most important one, so don't bury it under a vote count. End by putting the decision to the user.
